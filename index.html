How CyberSense works

CyberSense analyzes common indicators associated with phishing, fraud, and social engineering attacks.

Suspicious Links

CyberSense checks URLs for suspicious structures, shortened redirects, unusual domains, and lookalike websites.

Scam Messages

Messages are checked for urgency, fake rewards, financial requests, account warnings, and other common scam patterns.

Phishing Emails

Email content can be examined for suspicious senders, fake invoices, credential requests, and social-engineering techniques.

About CyberSense

Helping people stay safer online.

CyberSense was created to make phishing and scam detection easier for everyday users.

Online scams are designed to create urgency and pressure people into making quick decisions. CyberSense provides a simple way to pause, check suspicious content, and make a more informed decision before clicking or responding.

Why CyberSense?

Phishing attacks can affect anyone. A message may appear to come from a bank, delivery company, employer, friend, or online service. CyberSense provides a quick first layer of awareness by highlighting patterns that may indicate a scam.
